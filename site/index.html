Where the Shroud came from

The story is spread over 501 lore notes. Since 1.0 every one is voiced in English, German and Mandarin, and you can queue them like an audiobook.

492D.A.

  1. The Wanderer's gift

    A cloaked, sickly stranger brings Vorgoth a glowing blue flask and calls it a weapon the Ancients had hidden. Vorgoth drinks and feels himself lift off the ground. The notes call the stranger the Mysterious Wanderer.

  2. The Shroud rises

    The scholar Balthazar finds that the Elixir comes from tiny fungi deep underground. The same year a fog pours up from the depths. He comes to believe it is their spores, woken when the first Elixir Wells were dug.

  3. The capital falls

    Vorgoth leads Rimgard's pale, sick army into Meldore. They break into Pikemead's Reach and King Gormander is killed.

  4. The Ancients rise

    The Ancients swear never to take a side again, and the Shroud splits them: some will rise, some will stay. Balthazar writes that their capital now floats above the clouds, out of reach.

  5. The Cinder Vessels

    Young Ancients bring Balthazar a plan: guide an Ancient Flame into a human body to make someone who can drive back the fog. The Blacksmith volunteers to be the first to sleep in a Cinder Vessel.

  6. You wake up

    You climb out of a Cinder Vessel as a Flameborn. Light Flame Altars, rescue survivors and head north, all the way to Mother Flame's Domicile and Vorgoth.
